From planning to participation

As president of the College Student Union, I organized six campus events, with each serving roughly 30 to 300 attendees. The role involved coordinating preparation and keeping the team’s work moving toward a shared event.

Building continuity

I introduced biweekly reports and term-end handover summaries. These practices made ongoing work easier to follow and helped the next group inherit more than a list of unfinished tasks.
