The idea

From neural query policies to self-supervised error-reduction sampling.

Beginning with a query policy

The early phase explored neural-network-based query strategies for selecting useful training samples in a continuous sample space. The work connected sample selection with the behavior of the model being trained, and exposed questions about data requirements and continual learning.

Moving toward error reduction

I subsequently developed self-supervised error-reduction sampling. My résumé documents evaluation on an MLP-based DeepONet for Burgers’ equation, with attention to how the sampling approach affected learning.

What the experiments asked

The analysis considered sample efficiency alongside computational cost and continual-learning limitations. The two phases belong to one research trajectory: an initial strategy, the constraints it exposed, and a revised approach to choosing training data.
