An interactive starting point for my graduation project.

From exploration to a graduation project

This work is becoming my undergraduate graduation project. The current prototype brings a changing thermal field, component heating, and cumulative damage into one interactive environment, providing a starting point for a more rigorous investigation.

Making a process visible

The prototype lets a viewer inspect how a thermal process develops over time and how components respond. Its purpose at this stage is to make relationships and behavior visible enough to explore, compare, and discuss.

Current scope

The model currently uses simplified, uncalibrated demonstration parameters. Its visual behavior has not been physically validated, and it should be read as an exploratory prototype rather than a predictive engineering system.
