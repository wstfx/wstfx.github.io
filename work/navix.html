Starting with the family

I translate eldercare and family-support needs into product requirements and user journeys. The interaction work includes account permissions, AI conversations, and emergency-contact flows, connecting a family situation to the actions and information the product needs to support.

Making workflows tangible

I built Vue and TypeScript prototypes and a local scheduling service with seven MCP tools. Synthetic data, version-conflict checks, and retry handling allowed AI-assisted workflows to be demonstrated while exploring how the system should respond to changing or interrupted requests.

Research that informs decisions

I evaluated health-sensing and audio-service vendors, and organized 27 health-management sources into a searchable reference library. This work supports product design and integration planning; the prototypes are described as prototypes, without implying that every capability has been deployed.
