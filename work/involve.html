The idea

Designing the interaction between a lesson, a teacher, and a classroom.

A workflow built around teaching

InVolve brings lesson preparation, live classroom interaction, and response review into a connected workflow. The teacher interface is organized around Design, Teach, and Assess, with Prepare, Engage, and Practice phases within the lesson.

My contribution

I am primarily responsible for the frontend, UI, and interaction design. My work includes the teaching interface, question and question-set organization, student preview, and the transitions between preparing and distributing classroom content. A collaborator is responsible for backend development.

Keeping classroom state understandable

Draft questions stay out of the student lesson view until they are distributed. Student preview gives teachers a way to inspect the receiving experience, while question organization and statistics entry points help connect preparation, participation, and review.

Used in my own course

I created and delivered an introductory course on coding with AI agents through InVolve for people with no previous coding experience. This provided a real teaching context for the product, connecting my interface work with the task of helping beginners take their first steps.
