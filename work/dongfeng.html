The idea

Connecting model inference to reliable autonomous-driving evaluation.

Integration is part of the experiment

I integrated a vision-language-action model with Bench2Drive and CARLA for closed-loop evaluation. This required correcting trajectory coordinates and navigation mappings, and synchronizing camera inputs with ego-vehicle history so the model and simulator described the same state.

Tracing inference overhead

I identified and removed redundant multi-gigabyte array copies in the ONNX inference path. A route-level benchmark recorded approximately 68 seconds of baseline mean latency and 5.7 seconds of optimized warm-state median latency. These use different summary statistics and operating conditions, so they are reported as observations rather than a universal speedup ratio.

Making evaluation recoverable

I built a persistent PyTorch inference service and a resumable evaluation pipeline. Isolating simulator and model dependencies helped make the system easier to operate and diagnose, while distinguishing driving failures from simulator and evaluation faults made the resulting evidence more interpretable.
