The idea

A learning map for the rules of the road.

Organizing a body of rules

RoadLaw approaches preparation for the C1/C2 Subject One examination as a learning map. It organizes traffic regulations into a structured, interactive resource that learners can navigate.

My contribution

I independently developed the site. The project brings together website development, information organization, and a learning-oriented presentation of the material.
