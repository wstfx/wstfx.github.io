The idea

Teaching beginners to take their first steps with coding agents.

Starting from zero

The course was designed for people who had no prior coding experience. Its focus was using an AI coding agent to begin working with code, with explanations and exercises delivered through InVolve.

Teaching with a tool I helped build

I created the course and taught it through InVolve. This joined two responsibilities: shaping the learning material, and using the classroom interactions that I had worked on as the product’s frontend and interaction designer.

A concrete teaching artifact

The saved lesson includes written explanations, an exercise, and illustrated material. It provides a real teaching case alongside the software project, without treating sample classroom responses as evidence of actual student outcomes.
