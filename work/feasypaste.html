The idea

Keep the ideas. Keep the formatting.

A small but persistent frustration

An answer can be useful and still become difficult to use when copying it into another editor breaks its equations, numbering, or code. FeasyPaste addresses this handoff between an LLM response and a Feishu document.

The product

I independently developed the extension to convert LLM output into Feishu-compatible formatting. Its focus is a short, practical workflow: copy an answer and preserve the structure that makes it readable.

Designed around a concrete task

The project is intentionally focused on a recurring task. It reflects my interest in building small tools that remove repeated friction from learning and working with AI.
