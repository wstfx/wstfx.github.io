Research

Physical-field reconstruction baselines

Comparing reconstruction methods under changes in sensor trajectories, with attention to evaluation protocols and adaptation trade-offs.

Undergraduate Researcher · Westlake UniversityJan 2026–present

My contribution

Conducted baseline comparisons for physical-field reconstruction under sensor-trajectory shifts, evaluating randomized-trajectory fine-tuning and empirical Gaussian-process methods using shared evaluation protocols.

What I examined

Compared errors across scan and rotated trajectories, analyzing the trade-off between adaptation to new trajectories and performance on the original sensing distribution.
