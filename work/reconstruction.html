Reconstruction beyond one sensor path

Comparing physical-field reconstruction baselines when sensors follow trajectories different from the original sensing distribution.

The idea

Baseline evaluation under changes in sensing trajectories.

The evaluation question

A reconstruction method can perform differently when the sensing trajectory changes. My work examines this shift through baseline comparisons, using shared evaluation protocols to make the behavior of different methods comparable.

My contribution

I evaluated randomized-trajectory fine-tuning and empirical Gaussian-process methods, and compared reconstruction errors across scan and rotated trajectories. My contribution is centered on these baseline experiments and their analysis, rather than ownership of the overall paper method.

Reading the trade-off

The comparison considers both adaptation to new trajectories and performance on the original sensing distribution. This makes the trade-off visible: an adaptation strategy needs to be assessed for what it gains under a new trajectory and what it may lose under the original one.
