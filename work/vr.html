The idea

Connecting scientific accuracy with a VR learning experience.

From an abstract concept to an experience

The project explores how special relativity can be taught through an interactive virtual environment. My work drew on literature about embodiment, agency, and interactivity to inform the research design.

My contribution

I helped refine the instructional sequence and VR interactions, and verified relativistic parameters in Unity simulations. This connected the intended learning experience with the physical behavior being represented.

A research contribution with a defined scope

My contribution concerns research design support, instructional interaction, and scientific consistency. The work is presented on those terms, without claiming a measured improvement in learning outcomes.
