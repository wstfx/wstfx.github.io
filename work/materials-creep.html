The idea

Learning to build mechanical simulations around physical boundary conditions.

Working with a physical model

This work introduced me to mechanical simulation at the lattice scale and to the practical use of Abaqus. It expanded my research experience beyond model training into setting up physically constrained simulations.

My contribution

I worked on applying crystal-symmetry boundary conditions through coupling constraints. The experience emphasized how the definition of a simulation, including its boundaries, shapes the behavior it can meaningfully represent.
