The geometric fox is mathematically reconstructed from the supplied raster on a
30/60-degree lattice, with the original three colors and silhouette. It is not
the original vector source. Homepage scene drawings are editorial illustrations,
not product screenshots or scientific outputs. The story follows themes; the
About timeline holds chronology. Main has not been replaced by this prototype.

COMPANION ART
  assets/art/fox-mascot.svg       Five expressive poses, with head/eye/tail hooks
  assets/art/story-world.svg      Five conceptual chapter illustrations
  assets/art/tail-punctuation.svg The headline’s small fox tail
The builder inlines these SVGs so CSS can animate their parts. Static placement
transforms stay on wrappers, separate from animated groups. The world and fox
share one SVG coordinate system. Keep all illustrations readable without motion.
Asset content hashes are included in CSS/JS URLs to avoid outdated browser caches.
