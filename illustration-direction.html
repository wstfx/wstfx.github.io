ART DIRECTION V2 · 中文 / ENGLISH

One fox.
Different moments.

让同一只狐狸，经历不同的时刻。

用景别、视点、动作方向和主角权重制造变化，而不靠换一批道具。五幕依次是展开世界、靠近一个人的细节、俯看并选择、进入约束、退后让别人尝试。狐狸始终是同一个角色，但不必每次占据画面中心。

Create variety through framing, viewpoint, action direction and who holds the visual focus—not by swapping props around the same pose. The five beats are opening a world, attending to one person’s detail, looking down and choosing, entering constraints, and stepping aside for someone to try. The fox stays the same character without always occupying the centre.

供用户亲自创作 SVG 的第二版文字分镜；除保留并装饰开场原稿外，本文件不代表新插画已经制作或上线。

CHARACTER / VISUAL GRAMMAR

统一的是身份。

Consistency in character, freedom in viewpoint.

  1. 固定身份，不固定机位：小而尖的头、长腿、窄腰、挺立三角耳、细长鼻口、宽阔奶油色尾尖。改变透视时按同一个三维身体转动，不能把狐狸变成大头短腿吉祥物。

    Lock the identity, not the camera: a small pointed head, long legs, narrow waist, upright triangular ears, tapered muzzle, and a broad cream-tipped brush tail. Rotate one coherent body in perspective rather than turning it into a big-headed, short-legged mascot.

  2. 眼睛沿用原稿：小而睁开的有棱角水滴形，桃色虹膜、深色斜瞳、细小奶油色反光及原有额头高光。侧面可复用原路径；三分之四与俯视需要重新投影，不把一枚侧面眼贴在所有角度，也不强求背影视角露出眼睛。

    Preserve the original small open angular teardrop eye, peach iris, dark angled pupil, tiny cream catchlights and forehead highlight. The original paths work in profile; three-quarter and overhead views need a perspective-aware redraw. Do not paste a profile eye onto every angle or force an eye into a rear view.

  3. 用少量大曲面组织橙色、杏色、焦橙和奶油色，腿与耳背保持深绿。沿用原稿的柔和过渡可以，但不要增加碎面、毛发纹理、玻璃高光或金属质感。

    Organize orange, apricot, burnt orange and cream into a few broad curved planes; keep the legs and ear backs dark green. Subtle transitions from the master are welcome, but do not add tiny facets, fur texture, glass highlights or metallic finishes.

  4. 尾巴是动作的反向配重：抬头时向后舒展，前倾时向外展开，停下来时沿身体回卷。必须从身体自然长出并有完整根部，不能像一条孤立丝带；不要靠把尾巴越画越大来制造动感。

    Treat the tail as a counterweight: extend it behind a lifted head, open it against a forward lean, and curl it around a resting body. Give it a complete natural root rather than a detached ribbon; do not manufacture energy by enlarging it in every scene.

  5. 每张只保留一个主要动作和一个明确的视觉问题；通过不对称重心、身体倾角、前后遮挡产生动势。除开场外，不再连续使用“狐狸在左、桌子在右”的并排舞台。

    Give each image one main action and one legible visual question. Use an asymmetric centre of gravity, body tilt and overlap to create energy. After the opening, avoid a sequence of “fox on the left, table on the right” stages.

  6. 场景应与真实项目相连，但都是编辑插画：不虚构参与者外貌、听力辅助设备、手语姿势、访谈笔记、产品界面、模型成绩或课程成效。界面与真实证据另用获准的截图呈现。

    Connect the scenes to real work while keeping them editorial illustrations. Do not invent participant appearances, hearing devices, signed gestures, interview notes, product interfaces, model scores or teaching outcomes. Approved screenshots carry actual interface evidence elsewhere.

  7. 装饰围绕动作生长：纸路旁的细线、样本之间的短轨迹、路弯的切线、人物前倾形成的弧线。控制为一条主引导线、两簇次要纹样，不用每张都重复轨道圆、星星或漂浮图标。

    Let decoration grow from the action: fine lines beside the paper road, short sampling traces, a tangent at a bend, or an arc echoing a learner’s forward lean. Limit a scene to one guiding line and two small decorative clusters; do not repeat orbital circles, stars or floating icons everywhere.

  8. 动态图只是加强已经清晰的静态姿势。每幕最多三处独立运动，主要动作完成后留出明显停顿；避免人物嘴部或手部无休止摆动。关闭动画、移动端和首次加载时仍显示完整故事。

    Motion reinforces an already legible still. Use at most three independent moving parts per scene, with an obvious rest after the main action; avoid endlessly moving mouths or hands. The full story must remain visible with motion off, on mobile and during initial loading.

00 / Follow a question. See where it leads.

展开一条路：保留原稿，唤醒周围的空间

Unroll a path: preserve the original, enliven its surroundings

原稿已经把“一个小动作，打开一条可探索的路”讲得很清楚。保留狐狸、工作台、纸路和小车，不再重新生成。新增细线应使空白像有空气流过，而不是重新建一个装饰舞台。

The original already makes a small gesture opening an explorable route clear. Keep its fox, workbench, paper road and car. New line work should suggest air and continuity in the open space rather than build another decorative stage.

机位与动势 / Viewpoint and action

原有中远景与斜向纸路不变。这是五幕中唯一完整展示工作台的全景，用它建立世界，再让后面的画面走近或换视角。

Retain the existing medium-wide framing and diagonal paper road. This is the only full workbench view in the sequence: establish the world here, then move closer or change perspective.

构图层次与项目联系 / Composition and project anchors

前景 / Foreground

纸路下缘加一至两条断续的细弧线，沿卷纸的已有方向延伸，前段可稍深，尾部渐轻。线不能穿过狐狸或小车。

Add one or two interrupted fine arcs beneath the paper road, following its existing curve, slightly stronger near the action and lighter at the ends. Keep them clear of fox and car.

中景 / Middle distance

原稿几何全部保留。狐狸尾巴、小车、卷纸沿已有完整部件运动，不切开头颈。

Preserve all supplied geometry. Animate the already separated complete tail, car and paper curl; do not cut the connected head and neck.

背景与装饰 / Background and decoration

画布边缘两簇纤细芽叶，一簇低、一簇高，呼应原稿植物而不增加新花盆。再用一段稀疏弧线引导视线回到纸路，保持上方留白。

Place two slender sprout clusters at different heights near the canvas edges, echoing the existing plant without adding pots. One sparse arc brings the eye back to the paper road; leave the upper area open.

01 / Start with someone.

先靠近：一个人的细节值得停下来

Come closer: stop for one person’s detail

不再画两个人物隔桌交谈。把镜头推近到狐狸的三分之四侧脸、前胸和一只前爪；一位成年人的前臂从画外把打开的素色笔记本轻轻推近。狐狸从纸页抬眼朝向画外的人，耳朵朝向对方。重点是“我愿意靠近你的经验”，不是狐狸在阅读一份研究结论。

Replace the across-the-table conversation with a close three-quarter view of the fox’s face, chest and one forepaw. An adult’s forearm enters from outside the frame, bringing an open plain notebook closer. The fox lifts its gaze from the page toward the person beyond the frame, with ears attending in that direction. The moment says “your experience deserves attention,” not “the fox has read a research finding.”

机位与动势 / Viewpoint and action

近景，视点接近人的桌面高度，狐狸朝画面左上方微转约三分之四。头部占画面约四分之一，不改变头身真实比例；身体只是被景别自然裁掉。与开场完整侧身拉开距离。

Close view near desk height, with the fox turning three-quarter toward the upper left. The head occupies about a quarter of the picture through framing, not altered anatomy; the body continues naturally outside the crop. This deliberately differs from the opening’s complete side silhouette.

构图层次与项目联系 / Composition and project anchors

前景 / Foreground

打开的笔记本横切左下角，成人袖口与手只出现必要部分。纸页空白，无假笔记；保留一条干净书脊弧线。

An open notebook cuts diagonally into the lower-left corner, with only the necessary adult sleeve and hand visible. Pages are blank, with no invented notes; retain one clear spine curve.

中景 / Middle distance

狐狸面部与胸口在中右侧形成大而连贯的橙色体块，一只自然落下的前爪靠近书页但不压住它。视线离开笔记本，回到画外的人。

The fox’s face and chest make one large continuous orange mass at centre-right; a naturally resting forepaw sits near, not on, the page. Its gaze returns from the notebook to the person outside the frame.

背景与装饰 / Background and decoration

对方面部所在的左上方留白。仅用一条低对比、敞开的弧线呼应耳朵与书页，不画声波、气泡或“理解了”的符号。

Leave the upper-left area open where the other person’s face would be. One low-contrast open curve echoes ear and page; no sound waves, speech bubbles or symbols claiming understanding.

02 / Give curiosity something to push against.

换一个高度：下一次测量放在哪里

Change the viewpoint: where to measure next

俯视一片铺在地面的折展地形，狐狸绕到它尚未被描述清楚的一侧，前爪停在下一枚橙色取样点上方。已取样的几枚深绿小钉与一段短路径留在另一边。我们看见的是一次有依据的选择，不是狐狸隔着放大镜看玩具。

Look down onto a folded-out terrain surface on the ground. The fox curves around toward its less-described side, holding a forepaw just above the next orange sampling point. A few dark-green sample pegs and a short path remain on the other side. The picture shows a deliberate choice, rather than a fox peering through a lens at a toy.

机位与动势 / Viewpoint and action

约 60 度鸟瞰；狐狸从右下向左上斜切画面，背部、耳尖和弯曲身体形成近似逗号的姿态。尾巴反向展开，构成与上一幕近景完全不同的平面轮廓。

A roughly 60-degree bird’s-eye view. The fox crosses from lower right toward upper left, its back, ears and bent torso forming a comma-like silhouette. The tail opens the other way, creating a distinctly different shape from the preceding close-up.

构图层次与项目联系 / Composition and project anchors

前景 / Foreground

狐狸的后背和完整尾巴在右下，四肢透视要清楚；前爪伸向中央，不能多生一只爪。

The fox’s back and complete tail sit at lower right with coherent four-limb perspective; one forepaw reaches toward the centre without an extra limb.

中景 / Middle distance

不规则奶油色折展面横过画面，用三条宽阔鼠尾草绿等高带建立“场”。四枚以内已有样本，加一枚不同颜色的候选点；不是准确地图或实测结果。

An irregular cream fold-out surface crosses the frame. Three broad sage contour bands establish a field; use at most four existing samples and one differently coloured candidate. This is neither a precise map nor a measured result.

背景与装饰 / Background and decoration

让未取样区域安静留白；仅一条断续取样轨迹靠近已知点，不加坐标轴、网络、数据标签或大量等高线。

Keep the unsampled area calm and open. One interrupted trace stays near the existing points; no axes, networks, data labels or dense contours.

03 / Then, let the world push back.

进入弯道：让约束占据前景

Into the bend: bring the constraint forward

把小车和弯道推到近前，狐狸退到中景。一个路锥使直行路线不再成立；狐狸四肢着地、身体前倾，认真观察小车即将转弯的那一瞬。空间中的阻力比“狐爪扶着试验台”更直接。

Bring the car and bend into the foreground and move the fox into the middle distance. A cone makes a straight route impossible. With four grounded paws and a forward-leaning body, the fox watches the moment before the model turns. The constraint becomes spatially tangible rather than another paw resting on a test tray.

机位与动势 / Viewpoint and action

低机位、三分之四斜视；路面从左下近处斜向右上，再转向画面内部。狐狸位于弯道外侧、朝观众偏三分之四，不再是水平侧身。车在透视中明显比之前大，但仍是模型路面。

Low, oblique three-quarter view. The road starts close at lower left, runs toward upper right and bends back inward. Place the fox outside the bend, angled three-quarter toward the viewer instead of horizontally in profile. Perspective makes the car more prominent while the scene remains a miniature simulation metaphor.

构图层次与项目联系 / Composition and project anchors

前景 / Foreground

完整无品牌小车、一个路锥和宽而清晰的路弯形成近大远小。只保留一段简洁路面，不加城市。

A complete unbranded model car, one cone and a broad readable bend establish depth. Use one concise road segment, not a city.

中景 / Middle distance

狐狸越过弯道看向车辆，前肢一前一后稳定支撑；尾巴向外舒展平衡前倾。狐狸不驾驶、不拿方向盘、不做类人机械师。

The fox watches the vehicle across the bend with forelegs planted at slightly different depths; its tail opens outward against the lean. It does not drive, hold a steering wheel or act as a human mechanic.

背景与装饰 / Background and decoration

低对比的短路线引导车头；弯道外侧两三段短切线呼应“需要调整方向”，不要速度爆炸线、撞击符号或大片网格。

A short low-contrast route guides the car. Two or three short tangential lines outside the bend suggest adjustment; no explosive speed lines, collision marks or grid fields.

成年人位于中左，身体主动向键盘倾斜，一手落在键盘上，另一手自然停在旁边；笔记本屏幕背面主要朝向观众。焦点是姿态和动作，不是显示器内容。

CODA / LINE-ART BACKGROUND

给下一段路
留一点空间。

Leave room for the next path.

Understand more.
Make something useful.
Find the next question.

这一块的主角是三行字。线稿承担“故事还在继续”的余韵，不再讲一个完整项目；以不闭合的路径、前景与远处的尺度差形成呼吸，既能独立为静态背景，也可轻微流动。

The three lines of type are the protagonist here. Line art leaves the feeling that the story continues rather than introducing another full project. An open path and a foreground-to-distance scale change provide breathing room and work either still or gently moving.

文字保护区 / Keep the type clear

桌面建议以 1440 × 680 为母版：x=320–1120、y=95–510 为完全无图形的正文保护区。不是在字下画浅线，而是这一区域根本不放路径。实际文字超出时随内容扩大保护区；移动端使用单独裁切，上方或下方保留一条边缘线稿，正文与按钮区域全部清空。

For a 1440 × 680 desktop master, reserve x=320–1120 and y=95–510 as a genuinely empty type area. Do not merely fade lines beneath the letters: put no paths there. Expand this zone when actual text needs it. Use a separate mobile crop with line work on one outer edge above or below, keeping text and buttons completely clear.

  • 两级线宽：母版 2.2 px 外轮廓、1.1 px 内部必要线条；全图最多一处 3 px 的橙色短线强调。Web 展示小尺寸时可用非缩放描边或另做简化稿。

    Use two stroke weights on the master: 2.2 px outer contour and 1.1 px essential inner details, with at most one 3 px orange accent. At small web sizes use non-scaling strokes or a separate simplified drawing.

  • 线稿是有选择的轮廓，不是把每个色块边缘全部描出来。只保留耳朵、鼻口、背部、腿部姿势、尾巴外轮廓及一条胸口分界；后腿与尾巴重叠处可留断口。

    A line edition is a selective contour, not an outline of every colour-plane boundary. Keep ears, muzzle, back, leg pose, tail silhouette and one chest division; allow gaps where rear legs and tail overlap.

  • 使用鼠尾草绿或森林绿，最终网页透明度约 0.12–0.2 起步，橙色点缀略高但不能超过正文吸引力。不得借由叠加透明线在字后产生深色结点。

    Use sage or forest green, beginning around 0.12–0.2 opacity on the page. The orange accent may be a little stronger but must remain subordinate to the type. Avoid overlapping transparent paths creating dark knots behind text.

推荐：从这里，走向下一段路

Recommended: from here toward the next path

左下边缘是一只轻盈走开的狐狸的三分之四背影，仅用轮廓表达，头轻回望读者而非正面卖萌；尾巴宽阔地向左延伸。它前方的细路沿画布底边向右走，随后在右边缘向上拐出画面，始终绕开中央三行文字。右上角只有两片极简芽叶，像道路之外还有未展开的事物。路径不闭合，终点不画问号。

At the lower-left edge, a light rear three-quarter contour fox walks away, its head glancing back toward the reader without a front-facing cute pose; the broad tail extends left. A fine path ahead of it travels along the bottom edge, then turns upward and exits at the right edge, always clear of the central type. Two minimal leaves at upper right suggest something still to unfold. Keep the route open, with no question mark at its end.

从“Understand”到“Make”再到“Find”是一条继续向外的路。它与开场纸路呼应，又不重复完整插画；狐狸退居边缘，把结束语留给作者。

Understanding, making and finding form a route continuing outward. It echoes the opening paper road without repeating the full scene; the fox yields the centre to the author’s closing words.

PRODUCTION / PARALLEL CONTENT WORK

你把握主画面。
我把它带进网页。

You direct the illustration. I bring it into the site.

  1. 先做五张黑白剪影缩略图，按本稿顺序并排检查。把细节盖住后，五张仍应在景别、轮廓和行动方向上明显不同；如果看起来像同一张换了道具，先改构图。

    Start with five black-and-white silhouette thumbnails and compare them in sequence. With detail removed, framing, silhouette and direction should still differ clearly; if they look like one scene with different props, revise composition first.

  2. 确认第一张新的三分之四脸部与原稿一致，再完成 Notice。之后单独确认 Test 的俯视身体和 Return 的背影，避免一套错误透视扩散到全部章节。

    Approve the first new three-quarter face against the master before finishing Notice. Then check Test’s overhead body and Return’s rear view separately so an incorrect perspective does not spread through the set.

  3. 交付每幕的完整合成 SVG 与可编辑 AI 母版，并附一张无动画预览。可运动部件用本稿 layer id 命名；所有部件共用 viewBox，保留原始坐标。

    Deliver a complete composite SVG and editable AI master per scene, plus a still preview. Name movable parts with this brief’s layer IDs; share one viewBox and preserve original coordinates.

  4. 尾巴、纸角、小车必须是完整形状；被遮挡的根部与底面应补齐，提供 pivot 标记或坐标。无需拆开每个色块，也不必给相连头颈做人偶式骨骼。

    Tails, page corners and cars must be complete shapes. Finish hidden roots and underlying surfaces and provide pivot marks or coordinates. Do not split every colour plane or rig the connected head and neck like a puppet.

  5. 装饰线与主体分层，由网站整合阶段调整线宽、透明度和密度。桌面动效完成后生成同构图静态版，移动端与减少动态效果模式直接使用；动画尚未初始化时，插画和文字仍应完整呈现。

    Separate decoration from the main art so web integration can tune weight, opacity and density. Retain a same-composition still for mobile and reduced motion; art and text remain complete before the animation code initializes.

  6. 未来 Work/About 内容补充可并行进行：内容代理负责履历事实、项目叙述、证据与标签；视觉工作负责本分镜、SVG、样式与动效。约定一次只由一个代理运行全站生成脚本并提交，避免覆盖生成页面。

    Work/About content can proceed in parallel: a content agent owns experience facts, project narratives, evidence and tags; visual work owns these briefs, SVGs, styles and motion. Assign one integration owner at a time to run the site generator and commit, preventing generated-page overwrites.

交稿前检查 / Review before handoff

  • 去掉标题和动画，陌生读者仍能分别说出：展开、专注、选择、应对约束、让人尝试。

    Without headlines or motion, an unfamiliar reader can distinguish unfolding, attending, choosing, meeting a constraint and making room to try.

  • 五幕分别是中远景、近景、鸟瞰、低机位斜视、越肩背视，不是五张水平侧视狐狸。

    The sequence uses a medium-wide, close-up, bird’s-eye, low oblique and over-the-shoulder rear view—not five horizontal fox profiles.

  • 人与狐狸对话不再出现两次；Return 的主体是主动尝试的人。

    There are no two repeated fox-and-person conversations; Return centres a person actively trying.

  • 眼睛、耳形、鼻口、深绿长腿、色块衔接和奶油尾尖与作者原稿一致。背影视角不强行补眼。

    Eyes, ears, muzzle, long dark-green legs, colour-plane transitions and cream tail tip agree with the master; rear views do not force an eye into view.

  • 没有多出的腿爪，动作接触点合理，运动后不会露出空洞或断根。

    No extra limbs, plausible contact points, and no holes or detached roots after motion.

  • 插画能帮助理解标题，并提供项目线索，但不冒充真实界面、受访者、研究结果或课程成果。

    The illustration clarifies its heading and hints at the work without posing as actual UI, participants, research findings or teaching outcomes.

  • 大屏与 320 px 宽下动作都清楚；结尾线稿完全避开文字和按钮。

    Actions remain clear on desktop and at 320 px wide; coda line art stays entirely clear of type and buttons.

  • 动画暂停、系统减少动态效果、页面不可见和离屏时有正确静态或暂停状态；主文不会等待图片或脚本才出现。

    Motion pause, reduced-motion preferences, page visibility and offscreen states produce a correct still or paused state; primary text never waits for imagery or scripts to appear.
